The two leads

MEET THE CHARACTERS

Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. Meet the two protagonists of Grand Theft Auto VI, in Rockstar Games' own words.

JASON DUVAL

“Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder.”

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. After a stint in the Army trying to shake off his troubled teens, he found himself in the Keys doing what he knows best, working for local drug runners. It might be time to try something new.

LUCIA CAMINOS

“Fresh out of prison and ready to change the odds in her favor, Lucia’s committed to her plan — no matter what it takes.”

Lucia’s father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk. Life has been coming at her swinging ever since. Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary. Sheer luck got her out. Lucia’s learned her lesson — only smart moves from here.

Character biographies and artwork from Rockstar Games' official Grand Theft Auto VI site — confirmed details only, no leaks and no spoilers.

Take a closer look at the official Grand Theft Auto VI trailers.

Trailer 01 Released 4 December 2023

Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1

Rockstar Games' own description of the game, carried on the official trailer page: “Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.”

What We Know

  • Published by Rockstar Games on 4 December 2023 — the first look at Grand Theft Auto VI.
  • Introduces the game in the state of Leonida, with the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and locations beyond the city.
  • Introduces the story premise around Jason and Lucia.
  • Rockstar describes their story as a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida.

Details to Notice

  • Leonida is the setting, not Vice City alone: Rockstar points to locations beyond the city as well.
  • Rockstar's own framing puts the pair on “the darkest side” of Vice City as “the sunniest place in America”.
  • UnconfirmedNo shot-by-shot notes are given here: this card lists only what an official source states about the trailer.
